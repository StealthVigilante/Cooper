How a project runs

Six steps, the same team throughout.

Whether it is one keyless lock or a school campus, the process is the same. It is how we keep systems simple to use and easy to service.

  1. Step 1

    Conversation

    Call or send the form. We talk through the property, your goals and the timing.

  2. Step 2

    Site survey

    We walk the home or building, measure, and note what already exists.

  3. Step 3

    Design

    A system design and proposal built from proven components, with clear options.

  4. Step 4

    Installation

    Our own technicians run cable, mount, terminate and program everything.

  5. Step 5

    Handover

    A full lesson on your system, and how to expand it later on.

  6. Step 6

    Service

    Monitoring, maintenance and a number that reaches people who know your install.

Fewer switches. Fewer remotes. Fewer surprises.
