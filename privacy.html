Privacy Policy

This notice describes information handled through the Cooper Technology Group website and its project inquiry form.

Information we receive

The form collects your name, email address, town or city, and any phone number, project preferences or message you choose to provide. Please do not submit alarm codes, passwords, payment details or other sensitive security information.

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, and requests to the site to deliver the website and prevent abuse. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check for automated submissions.

How information is used and shared

We use inquiry information to respond, discuss your project and maintain related business records. The form does not subscribe you to marketing email or text messages. We do not use this website to sell personal information, serve targeted advertisements or track visitors across other websites.

Cloudflare provides hosting and spam protection. Resend processes submitted form information to email it to our designated business inbox; our email provider also processes and stores that message. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect people, rights and the security of our services. These providers may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Cookies, storage and external links

This website does not include advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts and website libraries are served from this website. A session-storage value coordinates page transitions and is not used for tracking. Cloudflare security services may use necessary browser storage or cookies to operate and prevent abuse. The website does not track activity over time across third-party websites and does not change that practice in response to a Do Not Track signal.

Links to mapping services and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy, Turnstile’s privacy information and Resend’s privacy policy for provider information.

Retention, security and your choices

Inquiry messages and related records are kept as needed to respond, provide requested services and meet legal or recordkeeping obligations. Provider logs, backups and email storage are subject to their respective settings and retention practices. We use safeguards appropriate to this website, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

To ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, or report a privacy concern, call (877) 266-7379 or write to Cooper Technology Group, 1603 Springtown Rd, Alpha, NJ 08865-4631. We may need to verify your identity and retain information required by law. The form is intended for people seeking business or home services, not for children under 13; we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this notice

Updates will appear on this page with a new effective date. For help accessing this notice or the form, please call the office.
