The approach

Connected home technology was once expensive, complicated and difficult to use. It doesn’t have to be.

Connected devices at home should make everyday life simpler. A glance at your phone shows the kids playing in the backyard. Your lighting, entertainment and security systems work together effortlessly. At Cooper Technology Group, we start by understanding your needs, design a system that works for you, and leave room to expand as your lifestyle evolves.

Convenience first

From your phone, arm your security system, adjust the lights or play your favorite music.

Connected home systems make everyday living simpler, giving you effortless control of your home whenever you need it.

Security first

See who is at the door, and know the garage is closed.

Others want certainty: live video of the front door, a text if the power fails and another when it is restored, proof that every door is shut.

Network connectivity

The network is the backbone of the connected home.

We design and install reliable, high-performance home networks that make the most of your internet connection. From hardwired connections to seamless Wi-Fi coverage, we ensure your devices stay connected with the speed and stability your home needs.

Using professional-grade networking solutions from Ubiquiti, we build secure, scalable networks designed to support today’s connected homes.

01

Security & Surveillance

Monitored burglary, fire, carbon monoxide and flood protection, with cameras placed from a survey of your property. We install and service thousands of systems across New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

  • Honeywell
  • Resideo
  • Kidde
  • Hanwha
  • Cellular radio
  • Smoke, heat, CO, glass break, motion, door, window, temperature and flood sensors
  • Hardwired, wireless or a mix, chosen room by room
  • Cellular communication, more reliable than phone or internet lines
  • Battery backup in the panel for power outages
  • Hardwired IP cameras to a local NVR, or Wi-Fi cameras where wiring is impractical
  • Doorbell cameras with instant alerts and two-way talk

Placement matters. A smoke sensor within three feet of a supply duct collects dust and trips false alarms. Details like this are why we install every system ourselves.
